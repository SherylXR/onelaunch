# Sarah — Demo Discovery Script & Expected Beats

## Who she is (for your pitch framing)
Sarah Lim, 48, ops manager, 16 years, retrenched May 2026 in a restructuring.
Pitch line: "Sarah is a composite of the thousands of PMETs retrenched in Singapore
last year." (Verify the current retrenchment stat from MOM's latest labour market
report before putting a number on the slide.)

## THE RULE
The pineapple tarts are NOT on the CV. They surface only in discovery.
That's the product's thesis performed live: CVs show what you did;
discovery finds what you are.

## Scripted answers (rehearse these — they look spontaneous on stage)

Q-type 1 — Career anchor probe ("what stung the most about being retrenched?")
ANSWER: "Honestly? The first week was awful. But part of me felt... free.
I'd been running someone else's playbook for 16 years."
→ signals: anchor=autonomy, sdt_autonomy high

Q-type 2 — Hidden strength probe ("what do colleagues come to you for that
ISN'T in your job description?")
ANSWER: "Untangling messy processes — people bring me chaos and I turn it into
a checklist. And outside work... every Chinese New Year I bake about 40 tins of
pineapple tarts for family and friends. I run it like a production line — my
nieces on pastry balls, me on jam, quality check before anything goes in a tin.
I lose whole afternoons and it never feels like work."
→ signals: flow=systematizing + baking, hidden_strength=process design applied
  to food production, S/E blend
→ THIS is the demo's pivot moment. Pause half a beat after she says it.

Q-type 3 — Autonomy vs income trade-off
ANSWER: picks autonomy-leaning option ("I've done stable. I want mine.")
→ autonomy_vs_income: autonomy_first
